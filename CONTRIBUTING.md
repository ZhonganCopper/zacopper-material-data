# Contributing and correction policy

This repository accepts publication updates from authorized maintainers and factual-correction reports from anyone.

Before adding a file, use the relevant template in [`templates/`](templates/) and ensure that it contains a product identifier, source type, revision, publication date, units, and limitations. Never publish customer-identifying information, personal data, confidential commercial terms, credentials, or unapproved internal records.

For corrections, provide the repository path, the statement or value in question, evidence for the correction, and the date observed. You may open an issue or contact the company through [www.zacopper.com](https://www.zacopper.com/).

Do not replace or delete a published value silently. Preserve the prior revision when practical and record the change in [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md).
