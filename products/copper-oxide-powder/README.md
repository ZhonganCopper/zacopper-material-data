# Electronic-grade copper oxide powder

Public technical resources for electronic-grade copper oxide powder.

**Official product and enquiry channel: [www.zacopper.com](https://www.zacopper.com/)**

## Publication checklist

- `specifications/`: approved public specification sheets, with grade and revision.
- `coa/`: example COAs or lot-specific COAs; never present an example as a guaranteed specification.
- `sds/`: complete current SDS files, including language and revision date.
- `particle-size-distribution/`: machine-readable PSD data plus sample, instrument, method, and provenance.
- `test-reports/`: reports whose scope, laboratory/testing unit, and date may be published.
- `application-notes/`: public use cases, conditions, and stated limitations.

## Interpretation

Do not use data in this directory as a replacement for a current, lot-specific COA or SDS. Values can vary by product grade, batch, test method, and sample preparation. Request current documents or a quotation at [www.zacopper.com](https://www.zacopper.com/).
